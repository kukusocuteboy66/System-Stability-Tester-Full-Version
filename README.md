<a id="overview"></a>

**System Stability Tester** is a powerful tool designed to assess the stability of your Windows system by calculating up to 128 million digits of Pi. Developed by **Lucas Tsatiris**, this utility employs advanced algorithms to ensure the integrity and performance of your computer. The **latest version** of System Stability Tester is always up-to-date with the newest features, making it an essential tool for anyone looking to benchmark their system effectively.

<a id="key-features"></a>

- **Calculation of 128 Million Pi Digits** — Perform extensive calculations to test your system's performance.
- **Multi-Process Support** — Utilize up to 32 processes for thorough stability checks.
- **CPU Recognition** — Automatically identify your CPU's model, brand, and operational frequency.
